FAQs

Before you enquire

Is THEGO accepting shipments now?

This is a pilot-stage prototype. Availability and the appropriate operating arrangement must be confirmed before any job is accepted.

Do you work with businesses and individuals?

The enquiry flow is designed for both. Business and repeat-service requests are a priority, subject to capacity and compliant arrangements.

How is the price decided?

Quotes depend on route, cargo size and type, timing, handling and partner availability. Agree the quote before acceptance.

Can you collect cargo directly at the airport?

This depends on cargo-release rules, the customer's authority and airport requirements. These must be checked for each shipment.

Interested in working with THEGO?

Businesses, logistics operators and delivery partners can introduce themselves and describe their coverage or requirements.
