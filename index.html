FAQ
Before you enquire




What does THEGO do?
We help coordinate airport cargo collection and onward delivery in Lagos, from the first enquiry through to the agreed handover.


Is my enquiry a booking?
No. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking. A shipment is accepted only after availability, price and terms are confirmed with you.


How is the price decided?
Quotes depend on route, cargo size and type, timing and handling needs. You agree the quote before the job is accepted.


Can you collect cargo directly at the airport?
That depends on cargo-release rules, your authority to collect and airport requirements. We check these for each shipment.


Do you work with businesses and individuals?
Yes. Business and repeat-service requests are a priority, and individual requests are welcome, subject to capacity.


Where do you operate?
We are starting in Lagos. Tell us your route in the form and we will confirm whether we can help.
